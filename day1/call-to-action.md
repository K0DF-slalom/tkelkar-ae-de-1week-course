# Day 1: Atomic Calls to Action

Small actions compound. The goal is to turn Day 1 motivation into a repeatable habit tomorrow and the next day.

## Instructor share (2-3 minutes)

Use this short script or adapt it:

"Before we wrap, pick one small action you'll repeat daily. It should feel almost too easy. We'll use it to build a habit loop."

1. Give 30-60 seconds of quiet time to choose a CTA.
2. Ask learners to post their choice in Slack or say it out loud.
3. Celebrate the small win and ask them to do it now or schedule it.

## Why this matters (Atomic Habits framing)

- Identity: saying "I'm the kind of builder who..." builds self-image, which drives behavior.
- Cue: a daily reminder makes the habit obvious and reduces friction.
- Small action: a 2-minute win creates momentum and makes repetition easy.
- Compounding: small, consistent actions beat occasional bursts of effort.

## The three calls to action

1. Identity statement: post one sentence in Slack: "I'm the kind of builder who ____."
   - Why: it ties the habit to who they want to become, not just what they want to do.
2. Daily cue: set a 2-minute reminder titled "Bootcamp: open repo + run first command."
   - Why: it makes starting obvious and lowers activation energy.
3. Small repeatable action: commit a single change (even a comment) or open a PR draft with a clear title.
   - Why: it proves "showing up" and builds confidence through visible progress.

## Next-day reinforcement

- Start Day 2 by asking: "Did your cue fire? What made it easy or hard?"
- Invite a 1-sentence reflection in Slack to keep the loop alive.
