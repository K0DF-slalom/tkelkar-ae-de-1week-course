# 1-Week Course — Day 5: Demo, Feedback & Next Steps

Day 5 closes the loop: demos, peer review, and a concrete plan to apply skills on real engagements.

## Outcomes

- Present a demo in a self-recorded video format with verification evidence.
- Give and receive actionable feedback on Codex usage and context engineering choices.
- Produce an “application plan” for the next 30 days.

## Capstone Demos (Self-Service)

- Submit a 5–10 minute self-recorded demo per participant or team.
- Demo expectations: The demo doesn’t need to be a formal technical presentation. Instead, focus on sharing your overall experience with Codex-—your key learnings, impact and achievements, biggest wins or ‘aha’ moments, challenges faced, and your feedback on the overall course content.
- Videos can be stored in the personal project repo
- Demo submission deadline: Friday 5 PM PST

## Review Checklist

- Does the repo/project include enough context to be repeatable by someone else?
- Are constraints and “definition of done” explicit?
- Is verification credible and easy to run?
- Are risks and limitations stated plainly?

## Application Plan (30–45 min)

- Pick 1–2 real tasks you will do “Codex-first” in the next two weeks.
- Define your guardrails (data policy, verification steps, review expectations).
- Identify the top 3 context assets you’ll reuse (templates, examples, scaffolding).

## Artifacts to Submit

- Demo recording repo link and the capstone README.
